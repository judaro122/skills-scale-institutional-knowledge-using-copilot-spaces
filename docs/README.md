# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

This repository organizes OctoAcme’s project management approach into a simple lifecycle that moves from initiation to planning, execution, release, and retrospective improvement. The goal is to give cross-functional teams a shared understanding of project outcomes, responsibilities, and quality expectations without requiring deep institutional knowledge from any single person. Each document in this folder builds on the others so that new team members can quickly understand the process, the decision points, and the artifacts that keep work aligned.

OctoAcme’s approach centers on clear ownership, iterative delivery, and customer value. Product and project leaders define priorities and track success, while engineering teams implement work in small, testable increments with explicit acceptance criteria. Communication is structured through regular team standups, stakeholder updates, and escalation paths for blockers or risks, which helps keep decisions visible and reduces ambiguity. Quality is treated as a continuous responsibility through review gates, testing, and Definition of Done checkpoints rather than a single final signoff.

The project management workflow starts with project initiation, where the problem statement, stakeholders, and high-level timeline are clarified. It then moves into planning, where backlog items, dependencies, milestones, and release timing are defined. During execution, the team delivers work incrementally, tracks progress, and monitors risk and quality. At release, the team verifies readiness, deploys, and communicates outcomes, while the retrospective phase captures lessons learned and improves the process for future projects.

The team’s roles are intentionally clear: Product Managers define goals and priorities, Project Managers coordinate delivery and communication, Developers build and maintain the solution, QA/Testing validates quality, and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Together, these roles create a repeatable model that balances speed, accountability, and operational transparency across the full project lifecycle.
